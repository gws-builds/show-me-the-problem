Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon entry

Show me the problem

A homeowner calls a plumbing or electrical business and points their phone at what is broken. An agent built on Meta's models sees the part, asks the one question that changes the price, quotes only from the owner's price list, escalates hazards, and books the visit after the owner taps approve.

Try it

Open this page on a phone with a camera, or on a laptop with a webcam. Point it at a dripping faucet, a water heater, a breaker panel, an AC unit, a thermostat, a disposal or a leaking pipe.

No broken faucet handy? Try it with our footage

A recorded clip plays as the camera. Its frames go to the models live, right now; nothing is pre-computed. You talk (or type) as the caller. Try: "My tap drips. What would it cost to fix, and when can you come?"

Uses SAM 3.1 (outlines the part and confirms hazards), Muse Spark 1.3 (decides with tools, points at parts, looks for hazards), Muse Voice Transcribe realtime (hears you hands-free) and Muse Image (a labelled illustration for the technician). Every price passes a verifier written in code. The business and its price list are fictional.
